(About Us)

Veranda is a boutique real estate agency partnering with leading developers to bring exceptional homes by the sea, in the green and in the city to buyers who expect more than a listing.

We work with a deliberately short list of developments. Every project is visited, tested and questioned before it reaches you, from title deeds and build quality to rental potential and resale.

From the first call to the handover of keys, and long after, one advisor stays with you: arranging viewings, coordinating lawyers and banks, and making a purchase abroad feel close to home.
